## KitVault Social Media & Marketing

Website:
https://kitvault.dev/

Primary marketing goal:
Drive developers to KitVault and get them to try the product.

Primary CTA:
Try it for free
https://kitvault.dev/

### Tools

For social media strategy, planning, hooks, captions, scripts,
carousels and platform optimization:
Use the installed social-media-skills.

For product demo and launch videos:
Use /brag and Hyperframes.

For AI-generated images or AI-generated video:
Use the OpenArt CLI available on the system.

Do NOT use:
- fal.ai
- Posta
- paid external APIs
- paid image generation APIs
- paid social scheduling services

OpenArt is already included in my existing subscription.

Before using OpenArt:
1. Check `openart account`.
2. Check model costs when appropriate.
3. Prefer models available under my existing OpenArt plan.
4. Never purchase credits, top up or activate another paid service.

### Brand

KitVault should feel:
- premium
- modern
- developer-focused
- technically credible
- clean
- useful
- commercial without looking spammy

Avoid generic SaaS marketing language.

Whenever possible, use the actual KitVault interface and real product
functionality instead of generic AI-generated UI.

### Output

Store marketing assets under:

marketing/social/

Organize by campaign and platform.

Never publish anything automatically.
Always prepare the final assets and copy for my approval.

### Social foundation (read first)

Before any social media or marketing work, read `marketing/social/foundation/`
(start with its README). It holds the brand profile, voice, audience, positioning
(including the claims register), content pillars, social strategy and per-platform
strategies. Every campaign must follow it.

Key correction to the notes above: KitVault's primary audience is **football shirt
collectors**, not developers. Developers are a secondary build-in-public audience
(mainly X and LinkedIn).
