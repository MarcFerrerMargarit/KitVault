# X Strategy — KitVault (supporting channel)
<!-- version: 1.0 · updated: 2026-09-23 · reads: brand-profile, voice, audience, content-pillars, social-strategy -->

## Role
Two lanes on one account:
1. **Kit culture (pillar 3 + 1):** Kit launches, leaks and "what season is this" debates happen
   in real time on X. We join as the account that knows the details.
2. **Build in public (pillar 4):** The solo-builder story for developers and indie makers.

Recommendation: run it as the **founder's personal account** with KitVault in the bio.
Personal accounts outperform logos on X, and both lanes fit one human. Alternatively, run
@KitVault for kit culture and the founder's account for building. Decide before launch.

## How X distributes (what we optimise for)
- Replies and conversation matter far more than likes. First-hour velocity decides reach
  outside your followers.
- **External links get throttled:** put kitvault.dev in the **first reply**, not the post.
- Premium materially boosts reach but is a **paid cost**. It's the founder's call and not
  assumed here (see the CLAUDE.md rule on paid services).

## Post types

| Type | Lane | Example shape |
|---|---|---|
| ID challenge (image) | Kit culture | Shirt photo, "Season? Wrong answers only. KitVault says 2003-04 at 88%." |
| Launch take | Kit culture | Quick, specific opinion on a new kit on launch day |
| Kit trivia | Kit culture | "The sponsor on this shirt only lasted one season. Why:" |
| Build log | Build in public | "Shipped bulk add: 20 photos, identified one at a time to stay under the rate limit. Here's the queue design." + screenshot |
| Honest number | Build in public | Real metrics only, once the founder provides them |
| Lesson | Build in public | "Greyed out paid plans instead of faking a checkout. Here's why." |

## Rules
- Native images/video in the post. Link to kitvault.dev in the first reply, with
  `utm_source=x`.
- 1–2 hashtags max, often none.
- 60/30/10 replies: 60% into bigger kit and football-shirt accounts' threads with genuine
  detail, 30% into indie-builder threads, 10% to our own followers. The human writes or approves
  every reply. No automation.
- No engagement bait, no pods, no quote-dunking on other collectors.
- Kit leaks: only share what's already public from reputable leak accounts, credit them, and
  never invent a leak.

## Cadence
3–5 posts/week + 10–15 min/day of replies. Kit-launch season (May–Aug) can go higher.

## Profile
- **Bio (founder account):** `Building @KitVault — your football shirt collection, finally
  organised. Photograph a shirt, AI identifies it. Try it for free:` + kitvault.dev
- **Pinned:** the best ID-challenge post, or the "why I built it" thread.

## Measure (native X analytics)
Replies, bookmarks, profile visits, link clicks, and UTM signups. Don't fabricate impressions.
