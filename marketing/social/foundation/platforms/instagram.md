# Instagram Strategy — KitVault ⭐ primary channel
<!-- version: 1.0 · updated: 2026-09-23 · reads: brand-profile, voice, audience, content-pillars, social-strategy -->

## Role
The main collector channel. Reels reach non-followers, carousels get saved and build authority,
and Stories keep warm followers engaged. The job is reach among collectors → profile visit →
bio link → try it free.

## How Instagram distributes (what we optimise for)
- Most reach comes from recommendations to non-followers. Reels and Explore are the reach
  surfaces.
- The top signals are watch time (the first ~3 seconds) and **sends** (DM shares). Saves matter
  for carousels.
- Eligibility: original content, **no TikTok watermark**, a clear niche. We are "the football
  shirt identification account", and that is what the algorithm should file us under.

## Format system

| Format | Share | Pillars | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reels (9:16, 1080×1920, 7–20s) | 2/week | 1, 2, 3 | Reach. The "Can KitVault ID this?" series lives here |
| Carousels (4:5, 1080×1350, 6–10 slides) | 1/week | 1, 2, 3 | Saves: season-ID guides, rankings, "my collection on a map" |
| Stories (9:16) | 3–4 days/week | all | Retention: "guess the season" polls, behind-the-scenes, repost our Reels |
| Static photo (4:5) | Rare | 3 | Shirt of the day, only with a great photo |

## Signature formats
1. **"Can KitVault ID this?" (Reel):** Hook frame: the shirt + "What season is this?" (1s). The
   viewer guesses. Screen recording of the photo going in, the fields filling, the confidence %.
   Reveal. Honest verdict (including misses). The end card asks the viewer to comment their
   guess. Built to be sent: "send this to the mate who thinks he knows kits".
2. **"My vault, filtered" (Reel):** One filter at a time (league → season → version) to a
   surprising result. "Every GK shirt I own: 4."
3. **Season-ID carousel:** "How to date a [club] shirt": slide 1 hook, slides 2–7 one detail
   each, final slide "Or photograph it — Try it for free".
4. **Map carousel/Reel:** The collection map filling in, country count, "which country am I
   missing?".

## Profile (conversion layer)
- **Name field (searchable):** `KitVault · Football Shirt Collection`
- **Bio draft:** `Your football shirt collection, finally organised. / Photograph a shirt — AI
  identifies it. / Try it for free ↓`
- **Link:** kitvault.dev with `utm_source=instagram`
- **Highlights:** How it works · Can it ID this? · Map · Pro (waiting list)
- **Pinned:** the best "Can KitVault ID this?" Reel, a how-it-works Reel, and one save-worthy
  carousel.

## Captions & discovery
- Keyword-first first line (Instagram is search): "Football shirt identification — [Club]
  [Season]".
- 3–5 niche hashtags max, at the end: #footballshirts #classicfootballshirts
  #footballshirtcollection + one club/league tag. No #fyp-style tags.
- Alt text naming the shirts on every post.
- CTA pattern: comment prompt on most posts. "Try it for free — link in bio" on ≤1 in 5 as the
  main message.

## Engagement routine (human, ~15 min/day)
- Reply to every comment in the first hour. When a guess is right or wrong, name the detail.
- Leave 5 thoughtful comments a day on collector accounts (IDs, season trivia). Never "check out
  our app".
- Share good follower shirts to Stories only with permission.

## Measure (native Insights)
Sends per reach, saves, 3-second hold / average watch time, profile visits → link taps, and
UTM signups on the site. Review weekly and double down on the format that drives link taps, not
the one with the most likes.
