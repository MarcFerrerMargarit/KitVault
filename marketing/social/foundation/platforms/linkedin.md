# LinkedIn Strategy — KitVault (maintenance channel)
<!-- version: 1.0 · updated: 2026-09-23 · reads: brand-profile, voice, audience, content-pillars, social-strategy -->

## Role
Credibility and the build story, not collector acquisition. Few shirt collectors browse
LinkedIn in collector mode, but developers, indie makers and potential collaborators do. The
job: make the founder known as someone who ships a real, honest AI product, and catch the
developer-collectors who'll try it.

## Personal profile, not a company page
Personal profiles get most of the feed, and company pages get very little organic reach. Post
from the founder's profile. Create a company page only as a placeholder so the founder can
list KitVault as their work.

## How LinkedIn distributes (what we optimise for)
- **Dwell time** (people reading) and early, **meaningful comments**. Likes barely move it.
- **Links in the post body suppress reach:** put kitvault.dev in the first comment.
- Posts have a long shelf life (days to weeks). One good post a week beats five thin ones.
- A specific, first-person, lived POV stands out in a feed full of AI-written posts.

## Content (mostly pillar 4, some 1)

| Post type | Example angle |
|---|---|
| Decision post | "I greyed out my paid plans instead of faking a checkout. What that cost and why." |
| Technical lesson | "Gemini returned 429s under load. The user saw a raw error. Here's the retry message I ship now." |
| Product demo (native video) | 30s screen recording: photo → identified shirt, and the interesting engineering behind it |
| Document carousel (4:5 PDF) | "How a photo becomes a catalogued shirt" architecture walkthrough |
| Milestone | Honest numbers, only real ones, once they exist |
| Why this exists | The founder's own collection and the spreadsheet that didn't survive |

## Rules
- Hook in the first 2 lines (before "…see more"). Short paragraphs. No broetry, no "Agree?"
  bait.
- Close on the product, not the stack. For example: "If you own more than a handful of football
  shirts, try it for free — link in the first comment."
- Tech detail is welcome here (Next.js 16, Supabase RLS, Gemini, d3-geo). This is the one place
  it belongs.
- Never pitch in DMs or connection requests.

## Cadence
1 post/week (max 1/day ever). 5 thoughtful comments/week on indie-builder and AI-product posts.
Human only.

## Profile
- **Headline:** `Building KitVault — AI that catalogues your football shirt collection from a
  photo`
- **Featured:** kitvault.dev (with `utm_source=linkedin`), the GitHub repo, the best demo post.

## Measure (native LinkedIn analytics)
Members reached, comments, profile views, link clicks from the first comment (UTM). Don't
fabricate numbers.
