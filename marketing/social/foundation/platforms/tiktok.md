# TikTok Strategy — KitVault (secondary channel)
<!-- version: 1.0 · updated: 2026-09-23 · reads: brand-profile, voice, audience, content-pillars, social-strategy -->

## Role
Cheap at-bats with a cold audience. Every video is tested on non-followers, and follower count
doesn't drive reach. TikTok is also a search engine ("how to tell football shirt season"), so
videos keep getting found for months.

## How TikTok distributes (what we optimise for)
- **Completion rate (aim for 70%+) and rewatches** are the top signals. The first ~1 second
  decides everything.
- **Search:** Put the keyword in three places: spoken or on-screen text, the caption, and the
  first on-screen frame.
- Judge by patterns across 10+ videos, never by one flop.

## Format system (9:16, 1080×1920)

| Format | Length | Pillar | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Can KitVault ID this?" | 8–15s | 1 | The loop ending returns to the shirt, so it gets rewatched |
| Haul → catalogue | 15–30s | 2 | "Charity-shop haul, all 6 in my vault in 60 seconds" (real timing only) |
| Season-ID explainer | 20–40s | 1 | Search lane: "how to tell what season a football shirt is" |
| Kit takes / rankings | 10–25s | 3 | Launch season, tournaments |
| Photo Mode carousels | 5–8 images | 1, 3 | Extra at-bats: guides and rankings (reuse the IG carousels, re-cut for 9:16) |
| Build in public | — | 4 | Rarely here. TikTok is for collectors |

## Native, not reposted
- Never upload the IG Reel with its watermark, and never cross-post the TikTok export to IG.
- Same footage, **native edit**: faster cuts, TikTok-native on-screen text, trending-but-licensed
  audio only from TikTok's Commercial Music Library (a business account can't use most trending
  songs).
- Faceless is fine: hands + shirt + phone screen recording. Founder voiceover is optional. No AI
  voices.

## Profile
- **Name:** KitVault · Football Shirts
- **Bio:** `Photograph a football shirt. AI tells you what it is. Try it for free ↓`
- **Link:** kitvault.dev?utm_source=tiktok (link in bio needs a business account).
- **Series/playlists:** "Can KitVault ID this?", "How to date a shirt".

## Captions & discovery
- The caption reads like a search query: "Football shirt season identification — 1998 Nigeria
  away".
- 3–4 niche hashtags: #footballshirts #footballshirtcollection #retrofootballshirts + club.
  Never #fyp.
- Pin a comment asking for the next shirt to test. Reply to comments with video replies, which
  fuel the series.

## Cadence
3 videos/week, quality-gated. Mostly native re-edits of the IG Reel footage, plus 1 TikTok-first
idea a week.

## Measure (native TikTok analytics)
Completion %, rewatch, shares, traffic source (For You vs Search vs Profile), follows per video,
and UTM signups on the site.
