# Product Positioning — KitVault
<!-- version: 1.0 · updated: 2026-09-23 · derived from: brand-profile v1.0, the product code,
     and a scan of public collector apps -->

## Positioning statement
For **football shirt collectors** whose collection has outgrown their memory, **KitVault** is
a **collection archive** that **catalogues a shirt from a single photo**. Unlike spreadsheets
and form-first collector apps, **the AI fills in team, season, version and manufacturer, and
you just check it**. The result is a private, searchable vault with a map of everywhere your
collection comes from.

## One-liners (pick by context)
- **Default:** Your football shirt collection, finally organised.
  (The site's own headline. Keep it consistent.)
- **Mechanism:** Photograph a shirt. KitVault tells you what it is.
- **Speed:** From camera roll to catalogued collection, 20 shirts at a time.
- **Spanish:** Tu colección de camisetas, por fin organizada.

## The alternatives collectors use today (and our honest contrast)

| Alternative | What it's good at | Where KitVault is different |
|---|---|---|
| Memory / camera roll | Free, zero effort | You can't search it, and it forgets |
| Spreadsheet / notes app | Total control | You type every field, so most people abandon it at 30 rows |
| Form- or database-first collector apps | Deep detail fields, databases, social feeds | KitVault starts from your photo and skips the typing. Fewer fields, less friction |

We never name competitor apps in public content. The public contrast is always "a spreadsheet".

## What we are / aren't (stay honest)

| KitVault is | KitVault is not (today) |
|---|---|
| AI identification from a photo, with a confidence % and your review | An authenticator or fake-spotter |
| Filters: country, league, season, version, team search | A valuation or price guide |
| Collection stats + an interactive world map | A marketplace |
| Private by default, full account deletion | A public profile or social network. **Sharing isn't built yet** |
| Web app that works in a phone browser, EN + ES | A native iOS/Android app |
| Open source | A developer tool |

## Messaging hierarchy
1. **Lead benefit:** A catalogue that fills itself in. Snap it, check it, done.
2. **Support:** Find any shirt in seconds (filters). See your collection on a map. Bulk add 20
   at once (Pro).
3. **Reassurance:** You review everything. Manual entry always works. Private. Free to start,
   no card.
4. **CTA:** Try it for free → kitvault.dev

## Claims register (legal/credibility)
- ✅ Can say: "identifies team, season, version and manufacturer", "shows how confident it is",
  "free to start, no card", "up to 25 shirts free", "5 AI identifications a day on Free",
  "private", "delete everything anytime", "open source".
- ⚠️ Say only with care: "under a minute" (single add, AI available). "Works on your phone"
  (browser, not an app).
- ❌ Never: accuracy %, user counts, "unlimited" on Free, sharing, valuation, authentication,
  "official" or any club affiliation, "download the app".
