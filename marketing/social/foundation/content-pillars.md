# Content Pillars — KitVault
<!-- version: 1.0 · updated: 2026-09-23 · derived from: brand-profile v1.0, audience v1.0, positioning v1.0 -->

## The set at a glance
- ⭐ **Know your shirt:** identifying seasons, versions and details, with the AI in the loop.
  ~35% of the mix.
- **The organised collection:** collections as archives: maps, gaps, counts, collector habits.
  ~25%.
- **Kit culture & nostalgia:** the shirts themselves: stories, rankings, launches. ~25%.
- **Built in the open:** one person building KitVault, with real numbers and real problems.
  ~15%.
- Promotion isn't a pillar. The CTA rides on posts from pillars 1 and 2.

---

## ⭐ Pillar 1 — Know your shirt
- **Covers:** How to tell what a shirt is: season, version, player vs fan cut, supplier, sponsor
  eras. Includes the "can the AI get this one?" format.
- **Why we own it:** KitVault's core feature is identification. Every post here demos the
  product without being an ad, and the confidence % gives honest drama.
- **For:** Both collector segments. It teaches the growing collector and challenges the serious
  one.
- **Goal it serves:** Reach (people send ID debates to friends) + conversion (product demo).
- **Example angles:**
  1. "Can KitVault identify this?" series: obscure shirts, reveal the fields + confidence %,
     including the misses.
  2. "Same shirt, three seasons": the tiny differences (sponsor, collar, crest) that date a
     shirt.
  3. "How to tell player version from fan version in 10 seconds".
  4. "Guess the season" Stories quiz, answer from KitVault.
  5. "The AI said 2006-07. It was 2007-08. Here's the detail that gives it away."
  6. How to photograph a shirt so it identifies well (flat vs hung, light, crest visible).
  7. Supplier eras: when Kappa, Umbro, Lotto dominated.
- **Draws on:** Real screen recordings of `/collection` + the add-shirt modal, the founder's own
  shirts, the version colour system (Home/Away/Third/GK).

## Pillar 2 — The organised collection
- **Covers:** Collecting as an archive: counting, sorting, gaps, maps, milestones, storage and
  cataloguing habits.
- **Why we own it:** This is literally the product (filters, stats, the map). Nobody else in
  collector content talks about organisation as a craft.
- **For:** The serious collector (pride, gaps) and the growing collector (getting organised
  early).
- **Goal it serves:** Saves + conversion (the most natural pillar for "Try it for free").
- **Example angles:**
  1. "My collection on a map": which countries, which are missing.
  2. "Filtered my vault to every GK shirt. There are 4. That's a problem."
  3. Collection milestones: first 25, first 100 (Free → Pro waiting list, honestly).
  4. "The spreadsheet I abandoned at row 31" (relatable before/after).
  5. Storage and care basics: hangers vs folded, sunlight (value content, no product needed).
  6. "Stats Sunday": shirts / countries / leagues from the founder's vault.
  7. Collection challenges: one shirt per league, every home kit of one club.
- **Draws on:** The map view, StatsBar, FilterBar, bulk-add progress UI, the founder's
  collection.

## Pillar 3 — Kit culture & nostalgia
- **Covers:** The shirts as culture: design stories, iconic seasons, rankings, new kit launches,
  "shirt of the day".
- **Why we own it:** It's the audience's home turf. We earn credibility by knowing kits
  precisely, which is the same precision the product sells.
- **For:** Serious collectors, the widest reach pool.
- **Goal it serves:** Reach + follows + trust. Low CTA, light product (a shirt card may appear as
  it looks in KitVault).
- **Example angles:**
  1. "Shirt of the day": one shirt, one story, as a KitVault-styled card.
  2. Kit launch reactions in season (May–Aug): quick takes, pulled from the leak/launch news
     cycle.
  3. Ranking carousels: best away kits of a season, best GK kits of the 90s.
  4. "The shirt that made me start collecting" (founder + invite replies).
  5. Tournament moments: World Cup/Euros shirts (seasonal overlays).
  6. Bilingual LaLiga/Spanish-club lane.
- **Draws on:** Public kit history (verify facts: `content-research-and-sourcing`), photos of
  owned shirts. Never club logos as brand assets.

## Pillar 4 — Built in the open
- **Covers:** The founder building KitVault solo: decisions, technical problems, honest
  numbers, what's next.
- **Why we own it:** A real solo build with a public repo and a genuine story (Gemini quotas,
  429s, payments not open yet). Rare honesty in a hype-heavy space.
- **For:** Developers/indie makers (secondary). It also gives collectors a face to trust.
- **Goal it serves:** Trust + amplification + a trickle of developer-collectors.
- **Example angles:**
  1. "Why paid plans are greyed out": shipping Pro honestly before payments exist.
  2. How the AI quota works (per-user, global fuse, burst limit) and why.
  3. "The shirts Gemini gets wrong, and what they have in common".
  4. Drawing a world map server-side with d3-geo.
  5. Translating the whole app to Spanish without an i18n library.
  6. Weekly/monthly honest numbers once they exist (signups, shirts catalogued).
- **Draws on:** Git history, the code, real metrics (only once the founder provides them).

## Mix & balance
- **Signature pillar:** Know your shirt. It's the differentiator, posted most often, and every
  week.
- **Rough split:** 35 / 25 / 25 / 15. Platforms weight it differently (see `platforms/`):
  Instagram and TikTok lean on pillars 1–3, X splits 3 and 4, LinkedIn is mostly 4.
- **Promotion:** A post whose main job is "Try it for free" is ≤1 in 5, usually from pillars 1
  or 2. Every other post carries a soft CTA (bio link) at most.
- **Seasonality:** Pillar 3 swells in kit-launch season (May–Aug) and around tournaments.
  Pillar 2 swells in Jan ("organise your collection" new-year energy) and at end of season.
