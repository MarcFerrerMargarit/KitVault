Reel, understands what KitVault is in 3 seconds, and taps the link.

### Name field
```
KitVault · Football Shirt Collection
```
Instagram search reads the name field as well as the handle, so "Football Shirt Collection" puts
KitVault in front of people searching those words. Instagram only lets you change the name twice
in 14 days, so get it right the first time.

### Bio (≤150 characters)
```
Your football shirt collection, finally organised.
Photograph a shirt — AI fills in team, season, version. You check it.
Try it for free ↓
```
- Line 1: the site's headline, in British spelling (as `voice.md` requires).
- Line 2: how it works, plus the honest review step.
- Line 3: the CTA, pointing at the link.

Alternative bio (if you'd rather lead with the mechanism):
```
Photograph a football shirt. KitVault tells you what it is — and files it in your vault.
Free to start, no card.
Try it for free ↓
```

### Long description
Not supported on Instagram. The bio is all there is.

## CTA
**Try it for free** (link title + last bio line). In captions: "Try it for free — link in bio",
as the main message on ≤1 in 5 posts (see `foundation/brand-profile.md`).

## Keywords (for captions, alt text and Reel covers, since Instagram search reads them)
football shirt collection app · football shirts · football kits · football shirt identification ·
catalogue your kit collection · classic football shirts · retro football shirts · kit collection

Hashtags (captions only, 3–5): `#footballshirts #footballshirtcollection #classicfootballshirts
#footballkits` + one club or league tag.

## Banner / cover
Instagram has no banner. The equivalents are:
- **Highlight covers** (1080×1920, icon centred in the middle ~400px circle): #0d0d0f background
  with a #4ade80 line icon. Four highlights (from `foundation/platforms/instagram.md`):
  `How it works` · `Can it ID this?` · `Map` · `Pro`. The "Pro" highlight says "waiting list",
  never "buy".
- **Grid:** the first 3 pinned posts act as the banner. Keep their covers readable in the 3:4
  grid crop.
