## Recommendation: `@kitvaultdev` on every platform

`@kitvault` is **taken** on 3 of the 6 platforms (GitHub, X, TikTok; see below), so it can't be
the handle everywhere. Of the next options, `kitvaultdev` wins:

- It **is the domain** with the dot removed. Anyone who reads `kitvault.dev` on a video, a
  sticker or a caption can guess the handle, and the other way round.
- It fits every platform's rules (11 characters, no dot). X allows 15 characters and no dots.
- GitHub confirms it's free. X and TikTok show no profile under that name.

**The trade-off:** "dev" can read as "a developer tool" to a collector. The display name
(`KitVault · Football Shirts` and similar) and the first bio line do the explaining, so the
handle doesn't have to. If that bothers you, `@getkitvault` is the equally consistent
alternative. Pick one and use it everywhere. Don't mix them.

On platforms where `@kitvault` might still be free (Instagram, LinkedIn), **don't split the
handle** to grab it. One handle everywhere is worth more than the short one on two platforms.

### ⚠️ The TikTok name collision
A shirt-selling account with the name "Kitvault" already has an audience on TikTok, and collectors
are exactly who it sells to. What that means:
- Collectors may assume KitVault sells shirts, or is connected to that account. The TikTok
  display name and bio must say "collection app" in the first few words (`tiktok.md` does this).
- Never tag, duet or reply to that account. Don't use "kitvault" as a hashtag on TikTok, because
  it will mix the two feeds. Use `#footballshirts` and the niche tags from the foundation instead.
- If people keep confusing the two, that's worth raising with a trademark adviser. That's the
  founder's call, and this isn't legal advice.

## Fallbacks, in order (same on every platform)
1. `@kitvaultdev` ← recommended
2. `@getkitvault`
3. `@kitvaultapp` (fine, but ".app" suggests a native app, and KitVault is web-only)
4. `@kitvaulthq`

Instagram and TikTok also allow dots, so `@kitvault.dev` is a platform-specific extra option
there. It's an exact match for the domain, but it breaks consistency with X and GitHub, so it's
only for when the fallbacks above are all gone.

**Don't use** `@kitvault.app`. It's mentioned in `brand-profile.md`, but KitVault doesn't own
`kitvault.app`, and the product isn't an app-store app.
