# Concept 03 — Kit Crest

A solid green football shirt silhouette with the KitVault **K** knocked out of the chest. The
shirt shape is the lucide `shirt` icon the app already uses for its shirt placeholders.

- **Why it works:** It's the only concept that says "football shirts" without words. That's good
  for cold discovery on Instagram and TikTok, where strangers see the avatar next to a Reel.
- **Weakness:** The K becomes tiny. At 64px it's small, and at 32px it's gone, so the avatar reads
  as "a green t-shirt icon". That's generic, and close to many shirt-shop and merch logos. It also
  departs from the site's favicon, which splits the brand into two marks.
- **Best use:** Not recommended as the profile image. Could work as a sticker, a Story highlight
  cover icon, or merch.
