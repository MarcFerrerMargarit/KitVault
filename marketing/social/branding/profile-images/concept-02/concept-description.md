# Concept 02 — KV Monogram

**K** in white and **V** in brand green, which is the split of the wordmark ("Kit" white, "Vault"
green), on the brand's near-black. A green vault bar runs under both letters. The V is drawn to
match the favicon K's cap height, width and stroke weight, so the two read as one family.

- **Why it works:** It spells the brand more fully than a single K, and it feels
  editorial/dev-tool premium on dark platforms (X, GitHub dark mode, TikTok).
- **Light version:** Dark K, with a deeper brand green (#16a34a) for the V and the bar.
  #4ade80 is too pale to hold on a light background.
- **Transparent version:** The monogram sits on a dark rounded tile, so the white K stays
  readable on any background.
- **Weakness:** Two letters at 32px are each half the size of concept 01's single K. They're still
  readable, but less punchy. There's also less colour mass, so it stands out less in a feed.
- **Best use:** The secondary mark: GitHub org, the Product Hunt maker profile, dark-mode
  placements, and places where the green tile would clash.
