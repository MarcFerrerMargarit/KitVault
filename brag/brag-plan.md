# Brag Plan: KitVault (vertical ad)

## What is this app?
KitVault is a home for football shirt collections. You photograph a shirt, AI fills in team, season, version and maker, and the shirt lands in a filterable grid and on a world map. It's free to start.

## The angle
A premium Reels/TikTok ad aimed at one audience: people who collect football shirts. The first line calls that audience out directly. The ad promises the site's own line ("finally organized") and then proves it in three quick, benefit-first cards: AI does the typing, you find any shirt in seconds, and you see your collection on a map. It closes on the logo and a clean "Try it for free" + URL frame.

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
At 0.0s a wall of real kit tiles bursts in behind giant bold uppercase text: "COLLECT FOOTBALL SHIRTS?" The line lands on the downbeat with a heavy soft impact. At 2.1s (strong cue) it snaps to "FINALLY ORGANIZED." in accent green, with the KitVault mark above it.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **AI does the typing.** "SNAP A PHOTO." A Barcelona shirt photo drops into the real Add Shirt modal, which shows "Analyzing with AI…". The headline flips to "AI DOES THE REST." Team, season, version and maker pop in on consecutive beats and stay on screen, and the green "87% confidence" banner lands.
- **Find any shirt in seconds** (the site's own copy). Shirt cards cascade into a 2-column grid, a filter chip toggles on, and the counter reads 25 shirts.
- **See your collection on the map.** Countries light up green one after another with a push-in on Europe, plus a "Spain · 6 shirts" tooltip and "25 shirts · 9 countries".

## Outro / punchline
The KitVault logo slams in alone (about 1.3s). Then a clean cut to the final frame: "Try it for free" (huge, centered) with "https://kitvault.dev/" prominently below it in accent green. Nothing else is on screen. The final frame holds about 3.1s and the video ends on it.

## User flow worth showing
Photo into Add Shirt → AI fills the fields → shirt appears in the collection grid → Map view.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: premium Instagram/TikTok product ad, commercial and punchy
- Interpretation: The feature-card structure (feature name + proof) comes from app-store, but the user asked for bold ad energy, so the film copy is Oswald 700 uppercase at huge sizes instead of medium title case. Beat-driven slide/push transitions of 0.35–0.45s, punch-in scale moves, and no dead air. Every line slams in fast and then holds long enough to read.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 20.5s

Safe zones: all copy stays between y≈250 and y≈1420, with an 80px side gutter and extra right margin for the Reels button column. The bottom ~450px carries only decorative UI or glow and no text a viewer must read. The exception is the final CTA frame, which is centered vertically.

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background #0d0d0f with pitch stripes; surfaces #1a1a1f / #212128; borders #2a2a33 / #3a3a45
- Accent #4ade80; text #f5f5f7; muted #8a8a96
- Map ramp #166534 → #4ade80 → #bbf7d0
- Display font: Oswald 700 (uppercase); body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: the kit tiles / ShirtCard with the team-colour band, the Add Shirt modal, and the choropleth map

## Share copy (draft)
KitVault is now live. AI shirt identification, instant filters and a map of your whole collection — all in one place. Try it for free: https://kitvault.dev/

## Audio direction
- Role: punchy commercial bed with motion-matched accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9 (114.8 BPM, strong from its first beat). It is trimmed with a 1.07s media offset so beat 1 lands on frame 0. Volume ~0.5, fade out over the last 1.2s.
- Music cue guidance: preset `cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`. In video time (preset − 1.07s) the beat grid is 0, 0.52, 1.05 … (≈0.5225s). Strong-cue locks: 0.00 (hook), 2.11 (tagline), 15.79 (logo). Field fills land on consecutive beats 6.33 / 6.85 / 7.37 / 7.89 and are all held afterward. Map countries light up on a fast accent grid; they are not text.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle. Music RMS breathes the green stadium glow.
- SFX posture: moderate and motion-matched (heavy soft impact on the hook and tagline, drop on the photo, soft clicks on the fields, bong on the banner, card place on the grid, switch on the map toggle, soft impact on the logo)
- Restraint rule: no SFX on the CTA frame; let the music fade under it.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 0.0–2.1s
The kit wall (real mock-data tiles) bursts in from scale 1.25, blurred at the edges. "COLLECT / FOOTBALL / SHIRTS?" slams in at 0.0 and holds to 2.0.
Sequential/interaction: tiles fly in staggered on the first two beats
Audio: heavy soft impact at 0.0
Transition: hard cut with scale punch → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Promise — 2.1–4.7s
The KitVault mark sits above "YOUR COLLECTION. / FINALLY ORGANIZED." with the second line in green. The text slams in at 2.11 (strong cue) and holds about 2.4s. The tile wall behind it dims and drifts.
Transition: push up (0.4s) → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — AI does the typing — 4.7–9.5s
Headline "SNAP A PHOTO." holds 4.8–6.3, then flips to "AI DOES THE REST." (held 6.3–9.4). Below it, the real Add Shirt modal (scaled for vertical): the photo drops at 5.27 → "Analyzing with AI…" → the form, with fields popping on 6.33/6.85/7.37/7.89 and the banner "AI suggested these details (87% confidence)" at 8.43.
Audio: drop, 4 soft clicks, bong
Transition: push up → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Find any shirt in seconds — 9.5–12.6s
Headline "FIND ANY SHIRT IN SECONDS." The 2-column shirt grid cascades in on beats, with FC Barcelona highlighted in green. Filter chips sit above the grid, and one toggles on.
Audio: card place and slide
Transition: push up → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — See it on a map — 12.6–15.8s
Headline "SEE IT ON A MAP." The world map fills the middle and 9 countries light up. The camera pushes in on Europe and a "Spain · 6 shirts" tooltip appears. The subline reads "25 shirts · 9 countries".
Audio: switch on the map toggle
Transition: quick scale-out → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Logo — 15.8–17.1s
The KitVault mark and wordmark slam to centre on 15.79 (strong beat), alone.
Audio: soft medium impact
Transition: quick crossfade → Scene 7

### Scene 7 — CTA — 17.1–20.5s
"Try it for free" (huge, centered) and "https://kitvault.dev/" (large, accent green) settle by 17.4 and hold to the end (3.1s). Nothing else is on screen.
Audio: music only, fading out

**Music mood:** upbeat, confident, commercial
**Audio summary:** a driving bed from frame 0, with impacts on the hook and tagline, tactile UI sounds through the product demo, a logo hit, and a clean fade under the CTA.

Note: all on-screen collection data is the app's mock data. No real user data.
